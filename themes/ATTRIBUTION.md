# Theme attribution

Makaron Themes v2 combines palettes and wallpapers from earlier Makaron releases
and the [Omarchy](https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy) theme collection.

Omarchy is Copyright David Heinemeier Hansson and distributed under the MIT
License included in `OMARCHY-LICENSE`.
The palette implementations remain subject to the licenses of their upstream
theme projects:

- Everforest: https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest
- Tokyo Night: https://github.com/enkia/tokyo-night-vscode-theme
- Catppuccin: https://github.com/catppuccin/catppuccin
- Kanagawa: https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim
- Matte Black and Retro 82: Omarchy theme collection

Wallpaper files are sourced from the Omarchy repository unless noted otherwise:

- `glass-light/backgrounds/wallpaper.jpg`: Makaron `2026.06.21`
- `everforest/backgrounds/1-tree-tops.jpg`: Omarchy Everforest
- `tokyo-night/backgrounds/1-sunset-lake.png`: Omarchy Tokyo Night
- `catppuccin/backgrounds/2-waves.png`: Omarchy Catppuccin
- `kanagawa/backgrounds/1-kanagawa.jpg`: Omarchy Kanagawa
- `matte-black/backgrounds/0-ship-at-sea.jpg`: Omarchy Matte Black
- `retro-82/backgrounds/1-in-the-groove.jpg`: Omarchy Retro 82

The original image names are preserved to keep their provenance searchable.
