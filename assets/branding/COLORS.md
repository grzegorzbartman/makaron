# Makaron Brand Colors

Source of truth for brand colors. `palette.html` is the editable source of
`palette.png` - regenerate with a headless browser screenshot after edits.

| Role | Light | Dark | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0F172A` | - | logo, headings, glyphs (light mode) |
| Cream | `#FAFBFC` | - | logo on dark, glyphs (dark mode) |
| Blue | `#0D6EFD` | `#2B7DFD` | focus pill, volume highlight, links |
| Amber | `#F59E0B` | `#F59E0B` | update indicator, rare highlights |
| Red | `#EF4444` | `#F87171` | alerts (CPU/RAM/disk/battery) |
| Slate | `#64748B` | - | secondary text |

SketchyBar ARGB mapping lives in `configs/sketchybar/colors.sh`.
Wordmark font: Rajdhani Bold (Google Fonts), letters as SVG outlines.
